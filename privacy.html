Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Local-first

The extension runs entirely in your browser. Your following/follower lists, whitelist and settings never leave your device.

2. Pro activation

If you purchase Pro, the extension sends your license key and X handle to our license server solely to verify the purchase. This data is used only for license validation and is never sold or shared.

3. No tracking

We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or fingerprinting.

4. Permissions

The extension requests access to x.com only, to read your follower lists and perform the unfollow/block actions you explicitly start.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Paddle, our merchant of record. We never see or store your card details.

6. Your rights

Contact yibolei2@gmail.com for data deletion requests.
