Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Local-first

Cleanup actions (unfollow, soft block) run entirely in your browser, on x.com pages you open. Your whitelist and settings never leave your device.

2. One-click analysis

When you use one-click analysis, the extension sends the X username you entered to our analysis server (ip.xiaonezha.shop), which retrieves that account's public following/follower data through a third-party data API and returns the analysis to you. Analysis results are cached on the server for up to 24 hours so repeat lookups are faster and cheaper. We do not build profiles of analyzed accounts, and cached results are never sold or shared.

3. Pro activation

If you purchase Pro, the extension sends your license key and X handle to our license server (license.xiaonezha.shop) solely to verify the purchase. This data is used only for license validation and is never sold or shared.

4. No tracking

We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or fingerprinting.

5. Permissions

The extension requests access to x.com (to read your follower lists and perform the unfollow/block actions you explicitly start) and to our own servers listed above (analysis and license validation). It does not access any other websites.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Paddle, our merchant of record. We never see or store your card details.

6. Your rights

Contact vip.xiaonezha@gmail.com for data deletion requests.
