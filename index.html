FAQ

Is it safe for my X account?

Yes — it ships on Safe speed with conservative daily caps. Aggressive automation risks X account locks, so the defaults protect you. You confirm every batch before anything happens.

How do I get my Pro license?

After purchase, claim your license key with your checkout email on the claim page. Paste it into the extension's popup and click Activate — it binds to your X handle.

What data does the extension collect?

Almost none. Everything runs locally in your browser. The only data that ever leaves your machine is your license key and X handle, sent once to our license server to activate Pro. See the Privacy Policy.
